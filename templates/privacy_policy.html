Maxfiylik siyosati (Privacy Policy)

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 23-noyabr

Ushbu Maxfiylik siyosati StudyAi mobil ilovasi (paket identifikatori: com.fundev.uz) va https://study-ai.uz veb-platformasidan foydalanishda foydalanuvchi ma'lumotlari qanday yig'ilishi, ishlatilishi va himoyalanishini tushuntiradi.

1. Biz yig'adigan ma'lumotlar

  • Akkaunt ma'lumotlari: foydalanuvchi nomi (username), ism-familiya, ixtiyoriy email yoki telefon raqami, shifrlangan parol va tanlangan interfeys tili.
  • Ta'lim va faollik ma'lumotlari: yaratilgan kurslar, darslarni tugatish holati, test natijalari, XP ballari, streaklar, kunlik topshiriqlar, energiya balansi va sertifikatlar.
  • AI so'rovlari va yuklangan fayllar: AI ustozga berilgan savollar, kurs yaratish mavzulari hamda dars yaratish uchun foydalanuvchi ixtiyoriy yuklagan PDF matnlari.

2. Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish va AI ishlovi

Ma'lumotlar faqat shaxsiylashtirilgan kurslar, testlar, amaliy topshiriqlar tekshiruvi, ovozli darslar va ta'lim statistikasini taqdim etish uchun ishlatiladi. AI javoblari va darslarini yaratish uchun so'rovlar xavfsiz kanal orqali Google Gemini API xizmatiga uzatiladi. StudyAi foydalanuvchi ma'lumotlarini reklama tarmoqlariga yoki uchinchi shaxslarga sotmaydi.

3. To'lovlar va xavfsizlik

Ilova ichidagi xaridlar yoki obunalar faqat Google Play Billing orqali amalga oshiriladi; StudyAi serverlarida bank karta raqamlari saqlanmaydi. Barcha aloqa HTTPS/TLS orqali shifrlanadi va parollar xeshlangan holda saqlanadi.

4. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish huquqi

Siz istalgan vaqtda ilova ichidan (Profil → Akkauntni o'chirish) yoki veb-sahifa orqali (https://study-ai.uz/delete-account/) akkauntingiz va barcha ta'lim ma'lumotlaringizni butunlay o'chirib tashlashingiz mumkin.

Политика конфиденциальности

Последнее обновление: 23 ноября 2026 г.

Настоящая Политика конфиденциальности описывает, как приложение StudyAi (ID пакета: com.fundev.uz) и веб-платформа https://study-ai.uz собирают, используют и защищают данные пользователей.

1. Какие данные мы собираем

  • Данные аккаунта: имя пользователя (username), имя и фамилия, необязательный email или номер телефона, хэшированный пароль и выбранный язык.
  • Учебный прогресс: курсы, пройденные уроки, результаты тестов, баллы XP, серии дней (streak), ежедневные задания, энергия и сертификаты.

Данные используются исключительно для предоставления учебных курсов, проверки заданий и сохранения прогресса. Учебные запросы передаются в Google Gemini API для генерации уроков и ответов. Мы не продаем персональные данные третьим лицам и не передаем их рекламным сетям.

3. Платежи и безопасность

Любые цифровые покупки и подписки обрабатываются защищенной системой Google Play Billing. StudyAi не хранит реквизиты банковских карт. Передача данных защищена шифрованием HTTPS/TLS.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StudyAi (Android package: com.fundev.uz) and https://study-ai.uz (operated by FunDev) collect, process, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: username, first/last name, optional email address or phone number, securely hashed password, and preferred study language.
  • Educational Activity: enrolled and generated courses, completed lessons, quiz answers, practice submissions, XP points, daily streaks, quests, energy balance, and certificates.
  • AI Prompts & Uploaded Documents: messages sent to the AI tutor, course generation topics, and PDF files you voluntarily upload to generate study courses.

2. How We Use Information & AI Processing

We use your data strictly to deliver personalized AI courses, evaluate practice exercises, track learning streaks, and provide customer support. Study prompts and uploaded study text are sent securely to the Google Gemini API solely to generate educational responses. We never sell your personal data or share it with third-party data brokers or ad networks.

3. Payments & Security

When digital purchases or subscriptions are offered on Google Play, transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. StudyAi never stores raw credit or debit card details. All network traffic between the app and study-ai.uz is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS, and passwords are stored using Django's cryptographic hashing.

4. Children's Privacy

StudyAi is designed for learners in compliance with applicable laws. Parents or guardians who believe a child under the applicable age of consent provided personal data without permission may contact us or use the account deletion page to remove the account immediately.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full control of your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated learning data at any time directly inside the StudyAi app (Profile → Delete Account) or online at https://study-ai.uz/delete-account/.
